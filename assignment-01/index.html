My Top 5 Favorite Video Games

This list is my 5 favorite video games and why they are my favorites.

  1. Red Dead Redemption 2

    This game has a very memorable and emotional story along with high quality gameplay.

  2. The Last of Us

    Another highly memorable and emotional story, making it one of the best single player games ever made.

  3. Elden Ring

    This game's aesthetic and inuitive gameplay makes it one of my favorite games that I can play for hundreds of hours

  4. MineCraft

    A classic high quality sanbox game that I can come back and play over and over again.

  5. Overwatch

    One of the earlier games I owned that introduced me to the multiplayer genre with a very satisfying gameplay loop.
